Open fire of birch and pine. Cast-iron kettle always warm. Stories told in low voices.

The Loft

Sheepskin and linen. A skylight for watching the aurora on clear winter nights.

The Sauna

Wood-fired, lakeside. Plunge into cold water. Return to steam. Repeat until human again.
